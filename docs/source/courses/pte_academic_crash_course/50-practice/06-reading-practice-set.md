# 第一部分：完形下拉填空 (RW-FIB) 精析

**【题型规则回顾】**：篇幅 ≤ 200 词，每空独立下拉菜单。**每空 1 分，零倒扣分 —— 所有空格必须 100% 填满！**

## 题目 01 —— 睡眠与记忆巩固机制 (Sleep and Memory)

> The relationship between sleep and memory has been studied intensively over the past two decades. It is now well **(1)** ____ that sleep does not simply allow the brain to rest, but plays an active role in **(2)** ____ newly formed memories. During deep sleep, patterns of neural activity recorded during the day are replayed, a process which appears to **(3)** ____ the transfer of information from short-term to long-term storage. **(4)** ____ , studies in which participants were deprived of sleep after learning a task consistently show poorer recall the following day. The effect is **(5)** ____ pronounced for procedural skills such as playing an instrument.

| 空格 | 候选选项 (Options) |
|---|---|
| **(1)** | *established* · *establishing* · *establishment* · *establishes* |
| **(2)** | *consolidating* · *considering* · *constituting* · *constraining* |
| **(3)** | *facilitate* · *fabricate* · *fluctuate* · *formulate* |
| **(4)** | *However* · *Accordingly* · *Nevertheless* · *In contrast* |
| **(5)** | *particularly* · *partially* · *potentially* · *precisely* |

### 官方破题推导全程：

- **第 (1) 空：【答案 *established*】**  
  - *第一步（语法槽位）*：句式为固定被动结构 "*It is now well ____ that...*"（公认的是……），后接过去分词。*establishing* (-ing形式)、*establishment* (名词) 与 *establishes* (动词三单) 仅凭语法词性即可直接秒杀排除！纯语法秒杀。
- **第 (2) 空：【答案 *consolidating*】**  
  - *第三步（学术固定搭配）*：四个选项全是 -ing 动词。在神经认知科学领域，“**记忆巩固 (memory consolidation)**”是绝对的固定学术搭配！*considering* (考虑), *constituting* (构成), *constraining* (限制) 放入句中均语义不通。
- **第 (3) 空：【答案 *facilitate*】**  
  - *第一步与第三步*："*appears to ____ the transfer*" 后接动词原形。白天神经活动的重放对记忆向长期存储转移起到什么作用？显然是**促进、便利 (facilitate)**。*fabricate* (捏造), *fluctuate* (波动), *formulate* (构想制定) 均无法与 "transfer" 构成合理动宾搭配。
- **第 (4) 空：【答案 *Accordingly*】**  
  - *第四步（逻辑方向）*：前一句讲“睡眠有助于转移巩固记忆”；后一句讲“剥夺睡眠会导致第二天的记忆召回变差”。后句是前句的**同向因果顺承推论**！因此需要因果副词 *Accordingly* (相应地/因此)。*However, Nevertheless, In contrast* 均为反向转折词，直接排除。
- **第 (5) 空：【答案 *particularly*】**  
  - *第三步（语义色彩）*：修饰形容词 *pronounced* (显著的)。后文列举了乐器演奏这一特定技能，旨在特别强调特例，需要强化的特异性副词 *particularly pronounced* (尤为显著)。

**标准答案：(1) established · (2) consolidating · (3) facilitate · (4) Accordingly · (5) particularly**

## 题目 02 —— 海岸侵蚀与硬性工程防护 (Coastal Erosion)

> Coastal erosion is a natural process, but human activity has **(1)** ____ its rate in many regions. Sea walls and breakwaters protect the stretch of shoreline immediately behind them, yet they frequently **(2)** ____ erosion further along the coast by interrupting the longshore drift of sediment. This has led some engineers to **(3)** ____ hard defences in favour of approaches that work with natural processes, such as restoring salt marshes and dunes. Critics argue that these methods are slower and less predictable, **(4)** ____ they are generally cheaper to maintain and more resilient to extreme weather.

| 空格 | 候选选项 (Options) |
|---|---|
| **(1)** | *accelerated* · *accumulated* · *alleviated* · *appreciated* |
| **(2)** | *aggravate* · *alleviate* · *allocate* · *advocate* |
| **(3)** | *abandon* · *abundant* · *abandoned* · *abandoning* |
| **(4)** | *because* · *although* · *therefore* · *whereas* |

### 官方破题推导全程：

- **第 (1) 空：【答案 *accelerated*】**  
  - 现在完成时 "*has ____ its rate*"。人类活动导致侵蚀速率如何？人类活动加速了侵蚀 ➔ *accelerated* (加速)。*alleviated* (缓解) 属于反义干扰项。
- **第 (2) 空：【答案 *aggravate*】**  
  - 注意句中转折连词 "*yet*"！前文说海堤保护了身后的海岸，“*yet they frequently ____ erosion further along*”（然而却加剧了其他海岸段的侵蚀）。转折词决定了该动词必须是负面词 ➔ *aggravate* (恶化/加剧)。
- **第 (3) 空：【答案 *abandon*】**  
  - 第一步语法秒杀："*led engineers to ____*" 后接动词原形。*abundant* (形容词), *abandoned* (过去式), *abandoning* (-ing形式) 全被词性秒杀，仅剩原形 *abandon* (放弃硬质防御海堤)。
- **第 (4) 空：【答案 *although*】**  
  - 第四步逻辑核查。前半句批评这些方法更慢且不可预测（负面评价）；后半句指出它们维护成本更低且更具韧性（正面评价）。前后形成鲜明让步转折，排除因果词 *because / therefore*。在 *although* 与 *whereas* 之间，由于两分句主语均为同一种方法 (these methods)，同主语让步首选 *although*。

**标准答案：(1) accelerated · (2) aggravate · (3) abandon · (4) although**

# 第二部分：拖拽选词填空 (R-FIB) 精析

**【题型规则回顾】**：篇幅 ≤ 80 词，共用词汇池。**绝对不要从左到右硬做，优先把确定项填入，利用排除法缩小候选池！**

## 题目 03 —— 蜜蜂的太阳导航与生物钟 (Bees Navigation)

> Bees navigate using a combination of landmarks and the position of the sun. Because the sun **(1)** ____ across the sky during the day, a bee must **(2)** ____ for the time elapsed since it left the hive. Experiments have **(3)** ____ that bees kept in darkness for several hours still fly in the correct **(4)** ____ when released, indicating an internal clock of considerable **(5)** ____ .

**底部全局候选词汇池 (Word Pool)：**  
*compensate* (补偿) · *direction* (方向) · *demonstrated* (证实) · *moves* (移动) · *accuracy* (精确度) · *distance* (距离) · *suggested* (暗示) · *travels* (旅行移动)

### 官方破题推导（排除法实战演练）：

1. **第一波锁定：最确定的第 (3) 空**  
   "*Experiments have ____ that...*" 后面紧跟客观实验确证的事实，需要过去分词。选入 *demonstrated* (实验证实了……)，将该词从候选池中彻底剔除！
2. **第二波锁定：第 (2) 空固定搭配**  
   "*must ____ for the time elapsed*"，情态动词接原形，且与介词 *for* 构成严密搭配 ➔ 必选 *compensate for* (为流逝的时间做出补偿校准)！剔除 *compensate*。
3. **第三波锁定：第 (1) 空三单动词**  
   "*the sun ____ across the sky*"，主语是太阳单数，天体划过天空的固定搭配为 *moves across the sky*。剔除 *moves*。
4. **收尾两空：候选词仅剩 *direction, accuracy, distance***  
   - 第 (4) 空：朝正确的方向飞行 ➔ "*fly in the correct direction*"；
   - 第 (5) 空：生物钟的品质是精准度 ➔ "*an internal clock of considerable accuracy*"。

**标准答案：(1) moves · (2) compensate · (3) demonstrated · (4) direction · (5) accuracy**  
*(成功排除干扰项：travels, suggested, distance)*

# 第三部分：段落重新排序 (RO) 精析

## 题目 04 —— 斑尾塍鹬的非停歇越洋迁徙 (The Bar-tailed Godwit)

> **(A)** *This assumption was overturned in the 1990s, when satellite tracking revealed that individual birds regularly cross the entire Pacific without stopping.*  
> **(B)** *The bar-tailed godwit is a medium-sized wading bird that breeds in Alaska and winters in New Zealand.*  
> **(C)** *Such a journey requires the bird to roughly double its body weight before departure and to shut down parts of its digestive system in flight.*  
> **(D)** *For many years ornithologists assumed that it made this journey in several stages, resting and feeding at islands along the route.*

### 官方相邻对推导链条：

1. **首句代词排查法**：(A) 含有 *This assumption*；(C) 含有 *Such a journey*；(D) 含有代词 *it made this journey*。全部含有前置依赖指代，全被枪毙！**唯一能作为独立首句的只有 (B)**，完整引入鸟类名称与繁殖地背景 ➔ **首位：B**。
2. **寻找 (B) 的承接对**：(D) 中的 "*it made this journey*" 指代 (B) 中从阿拉斯加到新西兰的迁徙 ➔ **形成 B–D 相邻对**！
3. **寻找反驳对**：(A) 开头的 "*This assumption was overturned*"（这一假设被推翻）直接呼应 (D) 中鸟类学家的传统假设 (assumed) ➔ **形成 D–A 相邻对**！
4. **寻找细节深化对**：(C) 开头的 "*Such a journey*"（如此艰辛的旅途）指代 (A) 中卫星追踪发现的“中途不着陆横跨整个太平洋” ➔ **形成 A–C 相邻对**！

**满分排序：B ➔ D ➔ A ➔ C** (稳拿满分 3 分！零生物学知识纯机械破题)。

## 题目 05 —— 肖维岩洞壁画颜料涂抹谜题 (Chauvet Cave Paintings)

> **(A)** *Instead, they concluded that the pigment had been applied deliberately, most probably with a brush made of animal hair.*  
> **(B)** *When the cave paintings at Chauvet were first examined, researchers were struck by the uniformity of the pigment layer.*  
> **(C)** *They initially suspected that the pigment had been blown onto the rock through a hollow tube, a technique documented at other sites.*  
> **(D)** *Microscopic analysis, however, showed none of the spatter pattern that such a technique invariably leaves.*

### 官方相邻对推导链条：

- (A) *Instead, they...*（排除，有 Instead 和代词）；(C) *They initially...*（排除，有代词）；(D) *Microscopic analysis, however...*（排除，有 however）。
- **确定首句 (B)**：完整交代肖维岩洞背景与研究人员主体 ➔ **首位：B**。
- **初始假说承接**：(C) 的 *They* 指代 (B) 的 researchers，且提出最初假设 (*initially suspected*) ➔ **形成 B–C 相邻对**！
- **转折证伪证据**：(D) 以 *however* 引入显微镜反证，否定了 (C) 中的空心管吹绘技术 (*such a technique*) ➔ **形成 C–D 相邻对**！
- **修正替代结论**：(A) 以 *Instead*（取而代之）顺接 (D) 的证伪，得出最终正确结论 ➔ **形成 D–A 相邻对**！

**满分排序：B ➔ C ➔ D ➔ A** (经典学术破题范式：观察 ➔ 假说 ➔ 证伪 ➔ 修正结论)。

# 第四部分：阅读单选题 (MCSA) 精析

## 题目 06 —— 无酬家务劳动的经济学估值争议

> Attempts to measure the economic value of unpaid domestic work have produced estimates ranging from 15 to over 40 percent of GDP, depending on the method used. The replacement-cost approach values each task at what it would cost to hire someone to perform it. The opportunity-cost approach instead values the time at what the person doing it could have earned in paid employment. The two methods diverge sharply for high earners: an hour of childcare by a corporate lawyer is worth far more under the second method than the first. Neither approach is obviously correct, and the choice between them is ultimately a judgment about what the measurement is for.

**【题目题干】** What does the passage suggest about the two valuation methods?  
*(文章对这两种估值方法给出了何种暗示？)*

- **(a)** The replacement-cost approach produces consistently higher estimates than the opportunity-cost approach.
- **(b)** The large range in published estimates is partly explained by the choice of method.
- **(c)** The opportunity-cost approach has been shown to be more accurate.
- **(d)** Economists have now agreed on a standard method for valuing domestic work.

### 官方排除法推导：
- **(a) 绝对化颠倒干扰**：文中仅指出对高收入者两者差异极大，且机会成本法更高，选项中加入了绝对化词汇 "consistently"，属于颠倒事实。排除。
- **(b) 审慎适度选项 (正确项 ✅)**：原文第一句明确指出估值范围在 15% 到 40% 之间波动是“*depending on the method used*（取决于所用方法）”。选项使用了极为严谨审慎的学术语言 "*partly explained by*"，与原文完全等价！
- **(c) 与原文直接矛盾**：原文明确指出 "*Neither approach is obviously correct*"（两种方法都并非显然正确）。排除。
- **(d) 反向捏造**：原文末尾说明选择哪种方法最终是一种主观裁量，并未达成统一标准。排除。

**标准答案：(b)**

# 第五部分：阅读多选题 (MCMA) 负分排雷精析

## 题目 07 —— 玛雅低地城邦衰亡成因研究

> The decline of the Maya cities of the southern lowlands between roughly 800 and 950 CE was once attributed to a single cause, usually either warfare or agricultural exhaustion. Current explanations are more cautious. Sediment cores from lake beds in the region record a series of severe droughts during exactly this period, the most prolonged lasting several decades. This evidence is now widely accepted, but drought alone does not account for the pattern of abandonment: cities in the northern Yucatán, which experienced the same climatic conditions, continued to be occupied for centuries afterwards.
> 
> What distinguished the southern cities appears to have been their dependence on rainfall-fed reservoirs rather than groundwater. The northern cities had access to cenotes — natural sinkholes reaching the water table — which provided a supply independent of annual rainfall. Southern cities had constructed elaborate reservoir systems that were impressive in normal years but had no alternative source in extended dry periods.
> 
> Archaeologists have also pointed to political factors. Inscriptions from the final decades show an increase in recorded warfare between cities and, in several cases, the abandonment of dynastic monuments before the cities themselves were deserted. Whether this political fragmentation was a cause of collapse or a symptom of resource stress remains contested, and the available evidence does not clearly distinguish the two.

**【题目题干】** Which of the following are supported by the passage? (Select all that apply.)  
*(以下哪些选项得到了文章的支持？请选出所有符合项。)*

- **(a)** Severe droughts occurred in the southern lowlands between roughly 800 and 950 CE.
- **(b)** Drought is now regarded as a sufficient explanation for the collapse.
- **(c)** Northern Yucatán cities had water sources less dependent on rainfall.
- **(d)** Political fragmentation has been shown to be the primary cause of collapse.
- **(e)** Southern cities relied on reservoir systems that failed in prolonged dry periods.

### 选项逐一排查与克制决策：

| 选项代码 | 评判定性 | 原文确凿实证比对 |
|---|---|---|
| **(a)** | ✅ **必选！(+1分)** | 原文第一段明确记载：“*Sediment cores... record a series of severe droughts during exactly this period.*” |
| **(b)** | ❌ **坚决不选！** | 原文白纸黑字写道：“*drought alone does NOT account for the pattern of abandonment.*” 选项宣称其为 "sufficient explanation"，完全相反！ |
| **(c)** | ✅ **必选！(+1分)** | 原文第二段明确指出北部城邦拥有天然落水洞 cenotes，“*independent of annual rainfall.*” |
| **(d)** | ❌ **坚决不选！** | 原文第三段明确指出政治分裂是原因还是结果“*remains contested*（仍存争议）”，选项武断声称 "has been shown to be the primary cause"，绝对化错误！ |
| **(e)** | ✅ **必选！(+1分)** | 原文第二段末句明确记载南部蓄水池“*had no alternative source in extended dry periods.*” |

**标准答案：勾选 (a), (c), (e) ➔ 斩获净胜分 +3 分！**

:::{important} 多选题克制战术警示
请注意干扰项 (b) 和 (d) 的设计规律：它们均去掉了原文中的审慎软化词（如 "not alone", "remains contested"），换成了绝对化的词汇 ("sufficient", "shown to be primary")。
在考场上，**只有当你能用手指在原文中明确指出证据支持时才打钩**！若对 (e) 稍有迟疑，只选 (a) 和 (c) 稳拿 +2 分净胜分，永远好过盲目勾选导致倒扣 −1 分！
:::
