# 实战真题库：听写句子 60 句权威特训集 (Write from Dictation Bank)

:::{admonition} 题库设计初衷与实战价值
:class: important
这 60 个句子严格按照真实 PTE 考场 Write from Dictation 题目的**学术语域、句长梯度、语法结构及高频话题**深度编撰。
它们不是不可查证的考生碎片残忆，而是专门用来训练你的**底层听音意群切分能力、短时记忆容量以及学术高频词的无死角拼写**。每个句子均深度嵌入了考场失分率极高的学术拼写地雷词（如 *accommodation, curriculum, committee, maintenance, laboratory* 等）。

**考场实战铁律**：掌握了这 60 句的意群切分与拼写，你就已经攻克了 WFD 最核心的语料库！
:::

## 如何使用本真题库训练

1. **生成真实发音**：使用电脑或手机的朗读功能（TTS 语音），选择标准英音或美音，以正常语速（约 150 词/分钟）单次播放句子。
2. **严格单次听音**：只播放一遍！听音过程中右手悬空，**严禁动笔**；听完后 3 秒内飞速在草稿纸上写下核心实词首字母。
3. **文本框默写重构**：根据首字母还原完整英文句子，重点核查主谓单复数 `-s` 与过去式 `-ed`。
4. **查漏补缺**：对照下方的中文释义与拼写提示，将所有拼错的单词登记在 {doc}`../60-toolkit/04-error-log-method` 错题本中，每个词罚写 5 遍。

## 组别 A：基础短句（8–10 词，共 15 句）—— 目标：100% 满分稳拿

短句是考场上白送分的题目，必须做到肌肉条件反射般零失误：

1. **The seminar has been relocated / to the adjacent building.**  
   *【译文】* 研讨会已被重新安排在相邻的大楼举行。  
   *【考点词】* **adjacent** (相邻的，易漏 d), **relocated** (迁移)

2. **All assignments / must be submitted / before the stated deadline.**  
   *【译文】* 所有作业必须在规定的截止日期之前提交。  
   *【考点词】* **assignments** (复数 -s), **submitted** (双写 t)

3. **The library / will remain closed / during the public holiday.**  
   *【译文】* 图书馆将在公共假期期间保持闭馆。  
   *【考点词】* **library** (拼写 r-a-r-y), **holiday**

4. **Attendance at the laboratory sessions / is strictly compulsory.**  
   *【译文】* 实验课的出勤是严格强制性的。  
   *【考点词】* **laboratory** (实验课，四音节), **compulsory** (强制的)

5. **Students should consult their supervisor / about the research proposal.**  
   *【译文】* 学生应就研究开题报告咨询其指导教师。  
   *【考点词】* **supervisor** (-or 结尾), **proposal** (提案)

6. **The lecture notes / are available / on the departmental website.**  
   *【译文】* 讲座讲义可在学院系所网站上获取。  
   *【考点词】* **departmental** (院系的), **available**

7. **Registration for the autumn semester / closes on Friday.**  
   *【译文】* 秋季学期的注册将于周五截止。  
   *【考点词】* **semester** (学期), **autumn** (含不发音 n)

8. **The committee / will announce its decision / next week.**  
   *【译文】* 委员会将于下周公布其决定。  
   *【考点词】* **committee** (双 m，双 t，双 e)

9. **Several scholarships / are available / for postgraduate applicants.**  
   *【译文】* 有数个奖学金名额供研究生申请者申请。  
   *【考点词】* **scholarships** (复数 -s), **postgraduate** (研究生)

10. **The examination timetable / has been published online.**  
    *【译文】* 考试时间表已在网上公布。  
    *【考点词】* **timetable** (单数合成词), **published**

11. **Please bring your identification / to the registration desk.**  
    *【译文】* 请携带您的身份证明文件至报到处。  
    *【考点词】* **identification** (身份证明), **registration**

12. **The department offers / a comprehensive range / of elective courses.**  
    *【译文】* 该系提供广泛的选修课程供学生选择。  
    *【考点词】* **comprehensive** (全面的), **elective** (选修的)

13. **Accommodation is guaranteed / for all first-year undergraduates.**  
    *【译文】* 所有大一本科生均可获得住宿保障。  
    *【考点词】* **accommodation** (双 c 双 m！), **guaranteed** (易错拼为 garanteen)

14. **The tutorial groups / meet twice a week.**  
    *【译文】* 辅导小组每周会面两次。  
    *【考点词】* **tutorial** (辅导课), **twice**

15. **Participants received / a detailed questionnaire / before the interview.**  
    *【译文】* 参与者在面试前收到了一份详尽的调查问卷。  
    *【考点词】* **questionnaire** (双 n 结尾), **participants**

## 组别 B：核心中长句（11–14 词，共 25 句）—— 目标：85% 以上高命中率

考场最主流的命题长度，按“主语群 / 动词动作 / 状语修饰”三段式意群切分记忆：

16. **The university library / will extend its opening hours / during the examination period.**  
    *【译文】* 大学图书馆将在考试期间延长开放时间。  
    *【考点词】* **examination**, **period**, **opening hours**

17. **Researchers have concluded / that the preliminary findings / require further independent verification.**  
    *【译文】* 研究人员得出结论，初步研究结果需要进一步的独立验证。  
    *【考点词】* **preliminary** (初步的), **independent** (-ent结尾), **verification** (核实)

18. **The curriculum committee / recommended a substantial revision / of the assessment criteria.**  
    *【译文】* 课程委员会建议对评估标准进行实质性修订。  
    *【考点词】* **curriculum** (双 r 单 l), **substantial** (实质性的), **criteria** (复数标准)

19. **Government funding / for scientific research / has declined considerably over the past decade.**  
    *【译文】* 过去十年间，政府对科学研究的资助大幅缩减。  
    *【考点词】* **government** (保留 n), **considerably** (大幅度地)

20. **Students are advised / to consult the recommended reading list / before the lecture.**  
    *【译文】* 建议学生在听课前查阅推荐的必读书单。  
    *【考点词】* **recommended** (单 c 双 m), **consult**

21. **The maintenance of laboratory equipment / is the responsibility / of the technical staff.**  
    *【译文】* 实验室设备的维护保养是技术人员的职责所在。  
    *【考点词】* **maintenance** (无 ai), **responsibility**, **equipment** (不可数绝无 -s)

22. **Our analysis suggests / that the environmental consequences / have been consistently underestimated.**  
    *【译文】* 我们的分析表明，环境后果一直以来都被严重低估了。  
    *【考点词】* **environmental**, **consequences**, **consistently**

23. **The conference / will bring together colleagues / from twelve different academic disciplines.**  
    *【译文】* 本次会议将汇聚来自十二个不同学科领域的学术同仁。  
    *【考点词】* **colleagues** (-gue结尾), **disciplines** (学科)

24. **Participants in the study / were selected / according to clearly defined criteria.**  
    *【译文】* 该项研究的参与者是根据明确界定的标准选拔出来的。  
    *【考点词】* **participants**, **defined criteria**

25. **The lecturer emphasised / the importance of accurate referencing / in academic writing.**  
    *【译文】* 讲师强调了学术写作中准确文献引用的重要性。  
    *【考点词】* **referencing** (文献引用), **emphasised/emphasized**

26. **Enrolment figures / for the engineering programme / have risen significantly this year.**  
    *【译文】* 工程专业的注册入学人数今年出现了显著增长。  
    *【考点词】* **enrolment/enrollment**, **significantly**

27. **The thesis / must be submitted / in accordance with the faculty guidelines.**  
    *【译文】* 毕业论文必须严格按照学院指导方针规范提交。  
    *【考点词】* **thesis** (论文), **accordance**, **guidelines**

28. **Preliminary results indicate / a strong correlation / between attendance and examination performance.**  
    *【译文】* 初步结果表明，出勤率与考试成绩之间存在高度相关性。  
    *【考点词】* **preliminary**, **correlation** (双 r), **attendance**

29. **The administration has introduced / a new procedure / for processing late applications.**  
    *【译文】* 院系管理部门出台了一套处理逾期申请的新规程。  
    *【考点词】* **administration**, **procedure** (程序规程)

30. **Economic historians / continue to disagree / about the primary causes of the recession.**  
    *【译文】* 经济史学家们就经济衰退的核心成因依然各执一词。  
    *【考点词】* **historians**, **recession** (双 s 经济衰退)

31. **The survey was distributed / to approximately four thousand / undergraduate students.**  
    *【译文】* 该问卷调查被分发给了约四千名在校本科生。  
    *【考点词】* **approximately** (双 p), **distributed**

32. **Scientists have developed / a more efficient method / for measuring atmospheric pollution.**  
    *【译文】* 科学家们研发出一种测量大气污染的更高效率方法。  
    *【考点词】* **efficient** (双 f), **atmospheric** (大气的)

33. **The archaeological site was discovered / during the construction / of a motorway.**  
    *【译文】* 该考古遗址是在修建一条高速公路期间被偶然发现的。  
    *【考点词】* **archaeological** (考古学的), **construction**

34. **Independent assessors / reviewed the methodology / before the findings were published.**  
    *【译文】* 在研究成果发表之前，独立评审专家对研究方法学进行了严格复核。  
    *【考点词】* **independent**, **assessors** (双 s 双 s), **methodology**

35. **The bibliography / should include every source / referred to in the text.**  
    *【译文】* 参考书目应列入正文中所引用的全部文献资料。  
    *【考点词】* **bibliography** (书目), **referred** (双写 r)

36. **Considerable progress has been made / towards establishing / a viable alternative.**  
    *【译文】* 在确立切实可行的替代方案方面，目前已取得了实质性进展。  
    *【考点词】* **considerable**, **viable** (可行的)

37. **The faculty has recommended / that the existing regulations / be amended immediately.**  
    *【译文】* 学院方面建议应当立即对现行规章条例做出修正。  
    *【考点词】* **regulations** (规章), **amended** (修正)

38. **Undergraduate students / may borrow up to twelve items / at any time.**  
    *【译文】* 本科生在任何时间最多可同时借阅十二件图书资料。  
    *【考点词】* **undergraduate**, **borrow**

39. **The workshop will address / practical difficulties encountered / during fieldwork.**  
    *【译文】* 本次研讨工坊将重点解决在野外田野调查期间遭遇的实际困难。  
    *【考点词】* **difficulties**, **encountered** (遭遇), **fieldwork**

40. **A separate questionnaire / was issued to / the participating secondary schools.**  
    *【译文】* 一份独立的调查问卷已被发放给参与该项目的各所中学。  
    *【考点词】* **separate** (中含 a rat), **questionnaire**

## 组别 C：高难度长句（15–20 词，共 20 句）—— 目标：保底 65–70% 拿稳双大分

长句考核瞬时短时记忆极限，执行“**首尾锁定保底战术**”：保死开头和结尾的完整性，中间顺着语感回忆。

41. **The committee has recommended / that the existing assessment procedures be reviewed / before the next academic year.**  
    *【译文】* 委员会建议在下个学年开始之前，对现行的考核评估规程进行全面复审。  
    *【考点词】* **committee**, **assessment**, **procedures**

42. **Although the preliminary data appeared promising, / subsequent analysis revealed / significant methodological limitations.**  
    *【译文】* 尽管初步数据看起来前景良好，但后续分析却揭示出原研究存在多项重大方法学局限。  
    *【考点词】* **preliminary**, **subsequent** (随后的), **methodological**

43. **The department is currently seeking / to appoint a lecturer / with substantial experience / in quantitative research methods.**  
    *【译文】* 该系目前正物色聘任一位在定量研究方法和统计分析方面拥有丰富经验的讲师。  
    *【考点词】* **appoint** (任命聘任), **substantial**, **quantitative** (定量的)

44. **Students who wish to transfer / between academic programmes / must obtain written approval / from both departments.**  
    *【译文】* 希望在专业之间转系的学生，必须事先获得转出系与接收系的双方书面批准。  
    *【考点词】* **transfer**, **programmes/programs**, **approval**

45. **The government's decision / to reduce funding for the humanities / has provoked considerable criticism / from universities.**  
    *【译文】* 政府削减人文学科资助的决定，遭致了全国各大高校的强烈抨击。  
    *【考点词】* **humanities** (人文学科), **provoked** (引发), **criticism**

46. **Researchers investigating the decline in insect populations / have identified pesticide use and habitat loss / as the principal factors.**  
    *【译文】* 调查昆虫种群衰减的研究人员指出，农药使用和栖息地丧失是导致该现象的最首要诱因。  
    *【考点词】* **investigating**, **pesticide**, **principal** (首要的，区别于 principle 原理)

47. **It is widely acknowledged / that the relationship between economic growth and environmental degradation / is considerably more complex.**  
    *【译文】* 公认的是，经济增长与环境退化之间的关联比先前设想的要复杂得多。  
    *【考点词】* **acknowledged**, **degradation** (退化衰退)

48. **The library has invested / in a comprehensive collection of electronic journals, / which are accessible to all registered members.**  
    *【译文】* 图书馆投资引进了一批门类齐全的电子学术期刊，所有注册会员均可便捷检索查阅。  
    *【考点词】* **comprehensive**, **accessible** (-ible结尾)

49. **Candidates should note / that the examination will consist of two separate papers, / each lasting approximately ninety minutes.**  
    *【译文】* 考生须注意，该项考试将由两张独立的试卷构成，每卷作答时间约为九十分钟。  
    *【考点词】* **candidates**, **separate**, **approximately**

50. **The archaeological evidence suggests / that the settlement was occupied continuously / for nearly four hundred years / before being abandoned.**  
    *【译文】* 考古学证据表明，该定居聚落在此地连续繁衍生息了近四百年，随后才被遗弃。  
    *【考点词】* **archaeological**, **occupied** (占领居住), **continuously**

51. **Participants were asked / to complete a detailed questionnaire / concerning their previous academic experience / and expectations.**  
    *【译文】* 参与者被要求完成一份详尽的调查问卷，内容涵盖其过往的学术履历及对该项目的预期。  
    *【考点词】* **questionnaire**, **expectations**

52. **The faculty has emphasised / that the maintenance of academic integrity / is the responsibility of every university member.**  
    *【译文】* 学院反复强调，维护学术道德与学术诚信是大学社区每一位成员不可推卸的责任。  
    *【考点词】* **emphasised**, **integrity** (诚信正直), **responsibility**

53. **Recent developments in computational modelling / have enabled scientists / to predict the behaviour of complex systems.**  
    *【译文】* 计算机计算建模领域的最新进展，使科学家们能够以极高精度预测复杂系统的运作机理。  
    *【考点词】* **computational**, **modelling/modeling**, **behaviour/behavior**

54. **Although the proposal received broad support / from the academic staff, / several colleagues expressed reservations / about financial implications.**  
    *【译文】* 尽管该提案获得了教职工的广泛支持，但数位同行同仁仍对其财务预算的潜在影响表示了保留意见。  
    *【考点词】* **proposal**, **colleagues**, **reservations** (保留态度), **implications**

55. **The findings of the investigation / will be presented to the governing body / at its next scheduled meeting in November.**  
    *【译文】* 该项调查的研究结论将于十一月份举行的下一次理事会例行会议上予以呈报。  
    *【考点词】* **investigation**, **governing body** (管委会/理事会), **scheduled**

56. **Students are reminded / that the submission of work containing unacknowledged material / constitutes a serious disciplinary offence.**  
    *【译文】* 郑重提醒广大学生，提交包含未注明引用出处资料的作业构成了严重的学术违纪行为。  
    *【考点词】* **unacknowledged** (未注明的), **constitutes** (构成), **offence/offense**

57. **The research council has announced / that applications for the next round of funding / must be received before the deadline.**  
    *【译文】* 科学研究理事会已发布公告，下一轮科研资助基金的申请必须在三月底前送达。  
    *【考点词】* **council** (理事会), **announced**, **funding**

58. **Historians of science have argued / that the development of the microscope / fundamentally transformed the study of biology.**  
    *【译文】* 科学史学家们指出，显微镜的问世从根本上颠覆并革新了生物学与医学领域的研究范式。  
    *【考点词】* **microscope** (显微镜), **fundamentally**, **transformed**

59. **The university's environmental strategy / includes a commitment / to reducing carbon emissions / across all campuses.**  
    *【译文】* 该大学的可持续环境战略包含了一项庄严承诺，即在所有校区将碳排放量锐减百分之二十。  
    *【考点词】* **commitment** (单 m 双 m: com-mit-ment!), **emissions**, **campuses**

60. **It has become increasingly apparent / that the existing infrastructure is inadequate / for the growing number of postgraduate students.**  
    *【译文】* 日益显而易见的是，现存的基础设施配置已远远无法满足不断激增的国际研究生群体的需求。  
    *【考点词】* **apparent** (双 p), **infrastructure** (基础设施), **inadequate** (不足的)

## 考场疲劳抗压训练执行卡

:::{tip} 必须在疲惫状态下训练 WFD！

**强烈建议：将每天的 WFD 听写打卡放在晚上高强度复习的最后 15 分钟进行！培养在疲倦眩晕状态下精准捕捉首字母的机械抗压习惯。**
:::
