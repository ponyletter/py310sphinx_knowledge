# 实战真题库：朗读文本 15 篇权威特训集 (Read Aloud Bank)

:::{admonition} 题库设计初衷与实战价值
:class: important
本特训库收录的 15 篇学术短文严格按照真实 PTE 考场 Read Aloud 题目的**学术语料库（自然科学、社会科学、历史、经济、地理、公共卫生、科技、数字密集型）、词数规模（45–60 词）与复杂从句结构**深度编撰。

每篇短文均由教研专家预先标出了**官方标准意群停顿斜杠 (`/`)**，并针对中国考生最容易卡壳和读错的**生词重音、-ed/-s 词尾、辅音连缀及数字读法**给出了细致的中文排雷指南。
:::

## 如何进行高效率自测训练

1. **30–40 秒极限准备**：先遮住文中的斜杠，自己拿铅笔划出停顿气口；快速扫读数字与长词，默念第一句。
2. **掐表录音朗读**：听到开考信号后 1 秒内开嗓，**目标用时 25–28 秒**（约 130–150 词/分钟的匀速节拍）。
3. **回听自查复盘**：数一数自己有没有倒车自我纠错、句中停顿是否突兀、句尾声音是否发飘。

### 文本 01 —— 环境与城市规划 (Environment, 52 词)

> Urban planners / have long recognised / that green spaces / improve public health, / but the economic case / for preserving them / has only recently / been quantified. / Studies in several European cities / suggest that mature trees / reduce summer temperatures / by up to four degrees, / lowering both energy consumption / and heat-related illness.

- **【中文参考译文】** 城市规划者长期以来都认识到绿地能够改善公众健康，但保护绿地的经济价值直到最近才得以量化。针对数座欧洲城市的研究表明，成荫树木可将夏季气温降低多达四度，从而降低能源消耗和与高温相关的疾病。
- **【发音避坑指南】**
  - *recognised*：重音在首音节 **RE**-cog-nised，不要读成 re-COG-nised；
  - *quantified*：**QUAN**-ti-fied（量化）；
  - *consumption*：con-**SUMP**-tion；
  - *by up to four degrees*：连贯平滑输出，不要在数字处卡壳；
  - *heat-related*：复合形容词视为一个完整重音单元。

### 文本 02 —— 宏观经济学 (Economics, 54 词)

> The relationship between inflation and employment / has been debated / since the nineteen-sixties, / when economists first observed / that the two appeared / to move in opposite directions. / Subsequent decades / produced considerable evidence / against any stable relationship, / and most contemporary models / treat the connection / as conditional rather than fixed.

- **【中文参考译文】** 通货膨胀与就业之间的关系自二十世纪六十年代以来一直备受争议，当时经济学家首次观察到两者似乎呈反向变动。随后的数十年产生了大量反对存在稳定关系的证据，大多数当代模型将这种联系视为具有条件的、而非固定不变的。
- **【发音避坑指南】**
  - *nineteen-sixties*：顺畅读出 "the nineteen-sixties"；
  - *economists*：重音在第二音节 e-**CON**-o-mists；
  - *subsequent*：**SUB**-se-quent；
  - *considerable*：con-**SID**-er-able；
  - *contemporary*：con-**TEM**-po-rary；
  - *conditional*：四个完整音节，切忌吞音。

### 文本 03 —— 生物科学 (Biology, 49 词)

> Photosynthesis converts light energy / into chemical energy / stored in glucose, / releasing oxygen as a by-product. / Although the process / appears straightforward in outline, / the underlying biochemistry / involves dozens of enzymes / operating in precisely coordinated sequence, / and several of its steps / remain incompletely understood.

- **【中文参考译文】** 光合作用将光能转化为储存在葡萄糖中的化学能，并释放氧气作为副产物。尽管该过程在轮廓上看似简单明了，但其潜在的生物化学机制涉及数十种酶在精确协调的序列中运作，其数个步骤至今仍未被完全理解。
- **【发音避坑指南】**
  - *photosynthesis*：pho-to-**SYN**-the-sis（光合作用）；
  - *glucose*：**GLU**-cose；
  - *biochemistry*：bio-**CHEM**-is-try；
  - *enzymes*：**EN**-zymes；
  - *coordinated*：co-**OR**-di-na-ted；
  - *incompletely*：平稳读出中段音节。

### 文本 04 —— 历史学 (History, 56 词)

> The Industrial Revolution / began in Britain / in the late eighteenth century, / driven by abundant coal, / accessible capital / and a legal system / that protected commercial contracts. / Historians continue to disagree / about which of these factors / mattered most, / though few now argue / that any single cause / adequately explains / the timing of industrialisation.

- **【中文参考译文】** 工业革命于十八世纪后期始于英国，由充裕的煤炭、便捷的资本以及保护商业合同的法律制度所推动。历史学家们对于这些因素中哪一个最重要仍存分歧，尽管如今很少有人认为单一原因能充分解释工业化的具体时机。
- **【发音避坑指南】**
  - *eighteenth*：咬住舌尖的清辅音 /θ/；
  - *accessible*：ac-**CESS**-ible；
  - *commercial*：com-**MER**-cial；
  - *contracts*：作名词时重音在前 **CON**-tracts；
  - *industrialisation*：六音节长词，保持匀速滑过。

### 文本 05 —— 人工智能与科技 (Technology, 51 词)

> Machine learning systems / identify patterns in data / without being explicitly programmed / to look for them. / This flexibility / is also their principal weakness: / because the patterns they find / reflect the data they were trained on, / systems trained on biased records / reproduce those biases / with considerable efficiency.

- **【中文参考译文】** 机器学习系统在没有被明确编程去寻找规律的情况下即可识别数据中的模式。这种灵活性同时也是其主要弱点：因为它们发现的模式反映了训练所用的数据，在带有偏差的记录上训练出的系统会以相当高的效率重现这些偏见。
- **【发音避坑指南】**
  - *explicitly*：ex-**PLI**-cit-ly；
  - *principal*：**PRIN**-ci-pal；
  - *reproduce*：动词重音在后 re-pro-**DUCE**；
  - *biases*：两个音节 "BY-as-iz"。

### 文本 06 —— 社会学调研 (Sociology, 47 词)

> Survey research depends / on the assumption / that respondents answer honestly / and understand the questions / as the researcher intended. / Both assumptions / are frequently violated. / Careful questionnaire design / can reduce these problems / but cannot eliminate them, / which is why / many sociologists supplement surveys / with direct observation.

- **【中文参考译文】** 问卷调查研究依赖于受访者诚实作答并按照研究人员意图理解问题的假设。然而，这两种假设常常被打破。周密的问卷设计可以减轻这些问题，但无法彻底消除它们，这也是为什么许多社会学家通过直接观察来补充问卷调查的原因。
- **【发音避坑指南】**
  - *respondents*：re-**SPON**-dents；
  - *questionnaire*：ques-tion-**NAIRE**；
  - *eliminate*：e-**LIM**-in-ate；
  - *sociologists*：so-ci-**OL**-o-gists。

### 文本 07 —— 地质学 (Geology, 53 词)

> Sedimentary rocks form / when particles of sand, / silt and clay / accumulate in layers / and are gradually compressed. / Because each layer / records the conditions / prevailing when it was deposited, / geologists can read / a sequence of strata / rather as one reads / the pages of a book, / working downwards through time.

- **【中文参考译文】** 当沙粒、淤泥和粘土颗粒分层堆积并逐渐被压实时，沉积岩便形成了。由于每一层都记录了其沉积时所处的环境条件，地质学家可以像翻阅书页一样解读地层的层序，逆着时间脉络向下追溯。
- **【发音避坑指南】**
  - *sedimentary*：sed-i-**MEN**-ta-ry；
  - *accumulate*：ac-**CU**-mu-late；
  - *strata*：**STRA**-ta（地层）；
  - *gradually*：三个音节，严禁吞读成 "grad-ly"。

### 文本 08 —— 公共卫生 (Public Health, 50 词)

> Vaccination protects / not only the individual / who receives it / but also the surrounding population, / because each immunised person / interrupts a potential chain / of transmission. / This indirect effect / is substantial enough / that coverage above a certain threshold / can protect those / who cannot be vaccinated at all.

- **【中文参考译文】** 接种疫苗不仅能保护接种者个体，还能保护其周围的人群，因为每个获得免疫的人都会阻断一条潜在的传播链。这种间接效应十分显著，以至于接种率超过特定阈值后，甚至能够保护那些根本无法接种疫苗的人群。
- **【发音避坑指南】**
  - *vaccination*：vac-ci-**NA**-tion；
  - *individual*：in-di-**VID**-u-al；
  - *transmission*：trans-**MISS**-ion；
  - *threshold*：**THRESH**-old（门槛阈值）。

### 文本 09 —— 语言学 (Linguistics, 48 词)

> Children acquire their first language / without formal instruction / and with remarkable consistency / across cultures, / which suggests / that the capacity for language / is in some sense innate. / What remains contested / is how much of the structure / is already present / and how much / is inferred from experience.

- **【中文参考译文】** 儿童无需正式教学，且在不同文化背景中以惊人的一致性习得他们的母语，这表明语言能力在某种意义上是天生的。目前仍存争议的是，这种结构中有多少是先天具备的，又有多少是从后天经验中推断出来的。
- **【发音避坑指南】**
  - *acquire*：ac-**QUIRE**；
  - *innate*：in-**NATE**（先天的）；
  - *contested*：con-**TEST**-ed；
  - *inferred*：in-**FERRED**。

### 文本 10 —— 天文学 (Astronomy, 55 词)

> The light reaching us / from distant galaxies / left them billions of years ago, / so every telescope / is in effect / an instrument for looking backwards in time. / This is inconvenient / if you want to know / what the universe looks like now, / but invaluable / if you want to know / how it has changed.

- **【中文参考译文】** 从遥远星系抵达我们的光线是在数十亿年前出发的，因此每台望远镜实际上都是一台望向过去的时间仪器。如果你想知道宇宙现在的模样，这确实很不便；但如果你想探寻它是如何演化的，这便是无价的瑰宝。
- **【发音避坑指南】**
  - *galaxies*：**GAL**-ax-ies；
  - *instrument*：**IN**-stru-ment；
  - *inconvenient*：in-con-**VEN**-ient；
  - *invaluable*：in-**VAL**-u-able（注意：意为“无价的、极其宝贵的”，不是不值钱！）。

### 文本 11 —— 认知心理学 (Psychology, 52 词)

> Human memory / is reconstructive rather than reproductive. / Each time we recall an event / we rebuild it / from fragments, / and the rebuilt version / is influenced by what we have learned / since the event occurred. / This explains / why confident witnesses / are often mistaken, / and why confidence / correlates poorly with accuracy.

- **【中文参考译文】** 人类记忆是重构性的而非复印式的。每次回忆某件事时，我们都是根据碎片重新构建它，而重构后的版本会受到该事件发生后我们所学到新信息的影响。这解释了为什么自信的目击证人往往会出现错误，以及为什么自信度与准确性之间的关联度极低。
- **【发音避坑指南】**
  - *reconstructive / reproductive*：清晰展现重音对比；
  - *fragments*：**FRAG**-ments；
  - *correlates*：**COR**-re-lates（动词）；
  - *accuracy*：**AC**-cu-ra-cy。

### 文本 12 —— 建筑与工程 (Architecture, 49 词)

> Reinforced concrete / transformed twentieth-century building / by combining / the compressive strength of concrete / with the tensile strength of steel. / The result / was a material / that could span greater distances / and support greater loads / than either component alone, / at a cost / that made large structures widely affordable.

- **【中文参考译文】** 钢筋混凝土通过将混凝土的抗压强度与钢材的抗拉强度相结合，彻底改变了二十世纪的建筑业。其结果是诞生了一种比任何单一成分都能跨越更大跨度、承受更大荷载的新材料，且成本低廉，使得大型建筑的普及成为可能。
- **【发音避坑指南】**
  - *reinforced*：re-in-**FORCED**；
  - *compressive / tensile*：com-**PRESS**-ive / **TEN**-sile；
  - *strength*：辅音群 -ngth，不要念成 "strenth"；
  - *affordable*：af-**FORD**-able。

### 文本 13 —— 农业与土壤科学 (Agriculture, 54 词)

> Crop rotation / maintains soil fertility / by alternating plants / with different nutrient requirements / and different root depths. / Leguminous crops / are particularly valuable / because bacteria in their root nodules / convert atmospheric nitrogen / into forms / that other plants can absorb, / reducing the quantity of fertiliser / that must be applied externally.

- **【中文参考译文】** 作物轮作通过交替种植养分需求不同、根系深度各异的植物来维持土壤肥力。豆科作物尤其具有价值，因为其根瘤中的细菌能将大气中的氮转化为其他植物可以吸收的形式，从而减少必须从外部施加的化肥用量。
- **【发音避坑指南】**
  - *fertility / fertiliser*：fer-**TIL**-i-ty / **FER**-til-i-ser；
  - *leguminous*：le-**GU**-min-ous（豆科的）；
  - *nodules*：**NOD**-ules（根瘤）；
  - *nitrogen*：**NI**-tro-gen。

### 文本 14 —— 法律与判例 (Law and Policy, 51 词)

> Precedent requires / that similar cases / be decided similarly, / which gives the law / a degree of predictability / that citizens can rely on. / The difficulty / is that no two cases / are identical, / and deciding which differences matter / is itself a judgment / that precedent cannot wholly determine.

- **【中文参考译文】** 判例原则要求同案同判，这赋予了法律一定程度的可预测性，使公民得以遵循。困难在于没有任何两起案件是完全相同的，而判定哪些差异具有实质性意义，本身就是一项判例无法完全决定的司法裁量。
- **【发音避坑指南】**
  - *precedent*：**PRES**-e-dent（名词判例）；
  - *predictability*：pre-dict-a-**BIL**-i-ty；
  - *identical*：i-**DEN**-ti-cal；
  - *determine*：de-**TER**-mine。

### 文本 15 —— 数字与年代密集型特训 (Numbers-Heavy, 56 词)

> Between 1950 and 2020 / the world's urban population / grew from roughly 750 million / to over 4.3 billion, / an increase of more than 470 percent. / By the 2050s, / an estimated 68 percent of people / will live in cities, / with almost all of that growth / occurring in Asia and Africa.

- **【中文参考译文】** 1950 年至 2020 年间，全球城市人口从大约 7.5 亿激增至超过 43 亿，增幅超过 470%。到本世纪 50 年代，预计将有 68% 的人口居住在城市中，几乎所有的增长都将发生在亚洲和非洲。
- **【数字与年代黄金读法标准】**
  - `1950` ➔ "nineteen fifty"
  - `2020` ➔ "twenty twenty"
  - `750 million` ➔ "seven hundred and fifty million"
  - `4.3 billion` ➔ "four point three billion"
  - `470 percent` ➔ "four hundred and seventy percent"
  - `the 2050s` ➔ "the twenty-fifties"
  - `68 percent` ➔ "sixty-eight percent"
  - **实战铁律**：在 35 秒准备期内，**必须把这些数字的英文读法在脑海中完整过一遍**！绝不在朗读中途现场计算数字读法。

## 终极自检提示：影子跟读模仿 (Shadowing) 的降维打击

每天精读上述文章的同时，配合 5 分钟的英文音频**影子跟读（延迟半秒模仿原声音调节奏）**。影子跟读训练出来的语流起伏，正是机器算法在判定流利度满分时的核心声学指纹！
